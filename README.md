Port Azahar 3D is an original open-world crime game in real 3D, built with three.js from low-poly primitives with no textures and no network requests at play time. At dusk in the container-port city of Port Azahar — quay cranes, stacked container yards, a marina of yachts, an old-town grid and a lighthouse point — you play Nadia Ferrer, an ex-customs inspector turned fixer, taking jobs across the harbour while sodium streetlights come on over deep teal water and an orange sky.

Systems include drivable and stealable cars plus a slow forklift, traffic, pedestrians on the quay, a chase camera on wheels and a third-person camera on foot, simple collision, shooting, cash and respect, a garage, a street race along the quay, local saves, a live HUD with a canvas minimap, and the Policía Portuaria (PPA): wanted level 1–5 escalates from patrol cars to PIT units, roadblocks, the AZAHAR-1 helicopter with a spotlight cone, and tactical vans. Eight story jobs run from a container-yard pickup under watch and a marina yacht theft to crane-top cover and the customs-vault finale.

Controls: WASD / arrows to drive or walk, mouse to look, E to enter or leave a car, F to shoot, Shift to sprint, Space for handbrake, and M for missions. The game runs from static files in desktop Chrome.

## Run it

`node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080
